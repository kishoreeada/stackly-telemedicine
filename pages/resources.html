01How should I prepare for a video consultation?
Keep your main question, current medicines and any recent changes nearby. A quiet, well-lit place and a reliable connection are usually enough.
02What should I ask when I’m not sure what kind of care I need?
Start with what is changing, how long it has been happening and what you want help deciding. A care professional can guide the next step from there.
03What should I keep after a consultation?
Keep the plan, prescriptions, follow-up timing and any questions that still need an answer. The goal is to make the next conversation easier.
04When should I reach out instead of waiting?
If something is worsening, feels unusual for you or is affecting daily life, it is reasonable to ask for professional guidance rather than waiting for it to pass.
